About this app

Eggcase Finder spots the eggcases of sharks, skates and rays (mermaid's purses) in a photo and counts them. Everything happens on this device: your photos are never uploaded anywhere, and it works without signal once it has loaded.

It's a counting helper, not an expert. Check the boxes, and use the Shark Trust's Great Eggcase Hunt ID guide to name the species and record your finds.
